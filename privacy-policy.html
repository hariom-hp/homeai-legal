Privacy Policy

Reno • Last Updated: November 2026
🛡️ Zero-Sale Guarantee: Reno is designed with Apple privacy-first principles. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal photos or information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Reno ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard your information when you use the Reno mobile application and associated AI architectural rendering services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide AI-powered room and architectural design transformations:

  • User Content: Room, building and garden photographs you capture or upload, and any design instructions you type.
  • Design Settings: The room type, style and palette you choose for each design.
  • Identifiers: A random app identifier created when you install Reno, used to apply your free daily limit and link your saved designs. It is not your Apple ID, email, or advertising identifier.
  • Purchases: If you subscribe, Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) process your purchase status. We never see your payment details.
  • Location: We do not collect, request, or track your location.

3. AI Photo Processing & Privacy

Before your first design, Reno asks for your permission to send your photo and instructions for AI processing. Nothing is uploaded until you allow it. Your photo is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our AI processing servers (hosted on Fly.io and Google Cloud) and to the third-party AI image service they use, only to create your design. Your photos are not sold and are not used to train AI models. You can withdraw permission at any time in Profile > Privacy Choices.

4. Information Sharing

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers, and Reno contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. Data is processed only by the providers needed to run the app:

  • Apple and RevenueCat for in-app subscriptions.
  • Fly.io, Google Cloud and our third-party AI image service to create your designs.
  • Amazon Web Services to store backup copies of your saved designs.

5. Data Security

We enforce strict organizational and technical measures:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit.
  • AES-256 encryption for saved cloud storage assets.
  • Hardware-backed Secure Enclave / Apple Keychain storage on your device.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

Saved designs are kept on your device, with a backup copy in our private Amazon Web Services storage, until you delete them. Deleting a design removes it from your device and our cloud storage. You may permanently delete your account and all saved designs at any time within the app under Profile > Delete Account & All Data; this removes local data and every cloud copy the app uploaded. Our AI processing servers may keep temporary copies of submitted photos only as long as needed to deliver your result. Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to inspect, download, or request complete erasure of your data.
