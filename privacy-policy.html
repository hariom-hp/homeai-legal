Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Welcome to HomeAI ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Summary in Plain English: HomeAI is a creative photo redesign tool. We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not show third-party ads, we do not require account registration, and we do not sell your photos or personal data.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many other applications, HomeAI operates on a privacy-first, registration-free model:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, or social media handles.
  • No Advertising Trackers: We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or Unity Ads) or track your IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers).
  • No Financial Details: All subscription and credit purchases are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never see, receive, or store your credit card or billing details.

2. Information Processed to Provide the Service

To enable AI room, exterior, garden, and floor plan redesigns, HomeAI processes the following:

  • User-Selected Photos: When you take a photo with the camera or choose an image from your photo library, that image is securely compressed and transmitted via HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to our dedicated AI inference processing servers.
  • Ephemeral Image Processing: Your uploaded images are used solely in real-time to generate your requested redesign render. Once the generation is complete and returned to your device, the processing cache is purged. We never use your photos to train public foundational AI models.
  • In-App Masking & Prompts: Custom style parameters, room selections, and inpaint brush masks you create are transmitted only to instruct the AI render for your active generation session.
  • Anonymous Device Keychain Identifier: A randomly generated UUID is stored in your device's hardware Secure Enclave Keychain to keep track of your local credit balance and daily free generation refreshes without requiring personal login credentials.

3. Local Device Storage

All your saved designs and generated renders are saved locally on your device in the application's sandboxed Documents storage. You retain complete ownership of all designs generated using HomeAI.

4. Apple StoreKit 2 & Subscriptions

In-app purchases (Consumable Credit Packs and Pro Auto-Renewable Subscriptions) are handled exclusively by Apple. Apple provides our app with cryptographically signed verification tokens (JWS) to unlock Pro features and credit allowances without sharing your personal payment information with us.

5. App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) — Data Deletion

In strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), HomeAI provides an instant, in-app data erasure mechanism:

  • You can navigate to Profile & Settings > Delete Account & Data at any time.
  • Tapping this immediately purges your local design index, removes all cached images, resets your Keychain credentials, and sends a purge request to clear any associated cloud session records.

6. Device Permissions

HomeAI requests the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used only when you choose to take a photo of a room, exterior, or garden directly within the app.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used to let you browse and select an existing room or floor plan image to transform.
  • Save to Photos (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used only when you tap the "Save to Photos" button to export your final generated design.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

HomeAI does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such information.
