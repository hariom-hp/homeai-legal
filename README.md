## 🔒 1. Privacy Policy

HomeAI is built with a **strict privacy-first architecture** in full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (including Guidelines 5.1.1 and 5.1.1(v)), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

### A. Data We Do NOT Collect
* **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
* **No Third-Party Advertising Trackers:** The app contains zero advertising SDKs (no AdMob, Meta, or Unity Ads) and does not access or track your Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
* **No Financial Information:** All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely by Apple StoreKit 2. We never receive or store credit card or payment information.

### B. Data Processed for AI Generation
* **User-Selected Photos:** Photos captured via your camera or chosen from your photo library are transmitted over secure HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to our AI processing servers solely to generate the requested design.
* **Ephemeral Processing:** Uploaded images are processed in real time and are not retained once the generation is complete. We do not use your personal photos to train public foundational AI models.
* **Anonymous Keychain Identifier:** A random UUID is stored in your device's hardware Secure Enclave Keychain (`kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`) to manage credit balances and daily free refreshes without requiring personal accounts.

### C. Local Storage & Complete Data Deletion
* All generated designs and saved favorites are stored locally in your device's sandbox.
* **One-Tap Data Deletion (App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v)):** You can permanently delete all local designs, cached files, and reset Keychain tokens at any time by going to **Settings > Delete Account & Data** inside the app.

### D. Device Permissions
* **Camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription`):** Capturing room and building photos for AI redesign.
* **Photo Library (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`):** Browsing and selecting existing room photos.
* **Save to Photos (`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`):** Saving generated high-resolution designs to your device.
