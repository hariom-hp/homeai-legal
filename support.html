Frequently Asked Questions

How do credits and subscriptions work?

HomeAI provides daily free generations. For unlimited or higher-volume generations, you can purchase credit packs or activate a Pro subscription directly via Apple In-App Purchases.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app, tap the Pro / Crown icon or navigate to Profile, and tap Restore Purchases. Your active entitlements will synchronize automatically with Apple's servers.

How do I request account and data deletion?

Open HomeAI > Settings > tap Delete Account & Data. All local designs, cached files, and credentials are permanently purged immediately.
